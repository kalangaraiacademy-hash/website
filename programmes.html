Future Plan 2026 – 2030

Building on nine years of work, these are the programmes we plan to develop next. Partners can adopt any of them.

Sindhanai Sirpi After-School CentresExpand after-school learning to underserved communities — academic support, reading habits, life skills and a safe learning space.
AI & Digital Learning InitiativeIntroduce children and communities to Artificial Intelligence, digital skills and emerging technologies.
School & Science Day ProgrammesScience activities, exhibitions and educational events that encourage curiosity and scientific thinking.
Self-Help Group (SHG) InitiativeSupport women and community members through SHGs, financial awareness, skills and livelihood activities.
Nutrition & Malnutrition SupportProgrammes supporting child nutrition and vulnerable families facing nutritional challenges.
