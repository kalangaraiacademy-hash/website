About the programme

From Awareness to Government Service

Kalangarai IAS Academy introduces rural students and youth to the opportunities available through TNPSC, TNUSRB, SSC, RRB and NEET examinations — and then supports them all the way through preparation.

Beyond classroom teaching, students are supported with motivation, doubt-clearing, study guidance and career counselling, which helps them develop confidence, discipline and a clear preparation plan. The aim is to help every student move from learning to opportunity: government employment, higher education or professional education.

Many of our achievers are the sons and daughters of fishermen, farmers and daily-wage workers from tsunami-affected villages — and are now serving in police, revenue, education and the courts.

Free Model Examinations

Success needs more than subject knowledge: students must manage time, understand the exam pattern and handle pressure. Kalangarai conducts free model examinations for TNPSC Group IV and Tamil Nadu Police recruitment under real exam-like conditions.

2025

45-Day Intensive Coaching Programme

In 2025 Kalangarai IAS Academy ran a 45-day intensive programme for aspirants of government competitive examinations and NEET. More than 25 students covered General Studies, General Knowledge, Aptitude, Reasoning and Current Affairs, with NEET guidance in Physics, Chemistry and Biology, exam strategies and regular practice tests. The programme was inaugurated by Kalangarai Trust members.
