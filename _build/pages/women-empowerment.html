Self-Help Group (SHG) awareness programme

Stronger Together

As part of its community development work, Kalangarai Charitable and Educational Trust visited a village in Nagapattinam district to hold an awareness programme for rural women on Self-Help Groups.

Mr. V. Kamal explained what SHGs are, how women can come together to form groups and the benefits of collective participation — developing savings habits, accessing financial support, exploring income-generating opportunities and improving economic independence. Participants shared their questions and discussed how SHGs can help their families and community.

Our objective: to create awareness among rural women about Self-Help Groups and encourage them to use collective opportunities for financial stability, self-reliance and social empowerment.
