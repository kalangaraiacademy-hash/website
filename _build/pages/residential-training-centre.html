A Place to Stay, Study and Succeed

2023 marked an important phase in Kalangarai's journey with the launch of the Free Training Centre on 26 April 2023. The inauguration brought together senior government officials, community leaders, educationists and other stakeholders.

With the encouragement of the District Collector, TNPSC and TNUSRB training classes were launched and the centre became Nagapattinam district's first free residential competitive examination training centre — so that rural aspirants who live far away can stay and prepare full-time.

The residential facility was developed with significant support from former Minister for Fisheries K. A. Jayabal. Smart boards and classroom equipment were provided by N. Gauthaman, former Chairman of the Tamil Nadu Fisheries Development Corporation.
