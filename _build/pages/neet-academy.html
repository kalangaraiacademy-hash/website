Free 3-month NEET coaching programme

A Structured Approach to NEET Preparation

Kalangarai Charitable and Educational Trust organised a free three-month NEET coaching programme for academically deserving students of Nagapattinam Government Model Schools, designed to provide structured preparation, expert guidance and continuous academic support.

With the support of the then District Collector, free NEET coaching was also extended to students of Nagore Modern School and VDP College.
