From Academic Excellence to Public Service

With the Tamil Nadu Fishermen Welfare Association, Kalangarai held an Achievement and Appreciation Ceremony for Fisheries University students who won gold medals. Through the “Thanga Mangai” appreciation ceremony, District Collector G. Johny Tom Varghese, IAS, felicitated the gold medallists and opened a pathway for them to receive free competitive examination coaching at Kalangarai Academy.
