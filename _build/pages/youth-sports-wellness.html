2019 – 2026 · a continuing wellness initiative

International Yoga Day

Since 2019 Kalangarai has observed International Yoga Day with the active participation of volunteers and students, making wellness and mental well-being part of the educational journey. Competitive examinations demand concentration, discipline and the ability to manage stress — yoga helps students build those habits.

With Liberty Basketball Academy

Summer Basketball Coaching Camp

Initiated by founder Mr. V. Kamal — himself a basketball coach — and organised with Liberty Basketball Academy at the Sports Ground of the Nagapattinam District Collectorate. A large number of school students trained in basic skills, techniques, fitness, teamwork and match practice.

  • Sports uniforms provided to participating students
  • Prizes and certificates for participation and achievement
  • District Sports Officer Mr. Raja attended as special guest
  • Parents joined to encourage their children
Sports recognition

Meeting with Thiru. Dayanidhi Maran, MP

Kalangarai representatives met Thiru. Dayanidhi Maran, former Union Minister and Member of Parliament (Chennai Central), to seek official recognition and registration for Beach Volleyball as a sporting discipline, to promote the sport and create better opportunities for local players, and to encourage rural youth through sport.

1 April 2019 · Nagapattinam

Voter Awareness Boat Rally

Kalangarai IAS Academy organised a Voter Awareness Boat Rally from the Nagapattinam harbour area to promote democratic participation and 100% voter turnout. More than 40 participants — fishermen, students and representatives of fishing village panchayats — took part using fishing boats.

“Let Everyone Vote… Let's Build Good Governance!”
