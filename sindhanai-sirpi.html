NEW · FREE EVENING SCHOOL

Sindhanai Sirpi Evening School

Beyond Tuition. Towards Holistic Child Development.

A new beginning

“We do not focus only on what children learn; we focus on who they become.”

The Sindhanai Sirpi Evening School — our after-school centre that opens when the school day ends — focuses on the all-round development of underprivileged rural children. Beyond conventional tuition, it combines academic support with creativity, communication, confidence-building, life skills, technology awareness and well-being.

Our vision is that every child, regardless of socio-economic background, has the opportunity to learn beyond textbooks, discover their potential and grow into a confident, capable and curious individual.

How we teach

Our 10 Integrated Learning Models

Academic SupportSchool subject reinforcement, homework help, practice, revision and individual progress tracking.
Spoken English & CommunicationEveryday conversation, vocabulary and sentence formation to communicate with confidence.
Reading & StorytellingGuided reading, storytelling, newspaper reading, listening and group discussion.
Government Examination AwarenessAn early introduction to UPSC, TNPSC, TNUSRB, SSC and RRB careers and eligibility.
Creativity & ImaginationDrawing, painting, arts and crafts, creative writing and group activities.
Social-Emotional Learning & Life SkillsTeamwork, leadership, problem-solving, emotional awareness and decision-making.
AI & Technology AwarenessBasic AI concepts, computer awareness, digital learning tools and safe digital practices.
Sports & Physical Well-beingIndoor and outdoor games that build fitness, teamwork and discipline.
Values & CitizenshipValue-based stories, good habits, social responsibility and environmental awareness.
Nutrition Awareness & Healthy HabitsHealthy snacks, food variety, hygiene and balanced eating habits.
Our unique identity

A Holistic, Child-Centred Approach

Through Sindhanai Sirpi, Kalangarai addresses not only academic learning gaps but also barriers relating to confidence, communication, exposure and access to learning resources — preparing confident, creative, responsible and future-ready children from rural and underprivileged communities.
