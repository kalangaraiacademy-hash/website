Keerthana: Three Years of Persistence

TNPSC Group IV · Education Department

Keerthana, 25, from Kottar Vasapadi, lives with a visual disability; her father supports the family by selling bananas from a pushcart. She prepared at Kalangarai IAS Academy for three years, reaching the TNPSC Group II Mains and the Group IV posting stage more than once without an appointment.

She refused to give up. In 2025 she cleared TNPSC Group IV and received a government appointment in the Education Department.

“Kalangarai serves as a guiding light for many students like me. To me, Kalangarai is not just an organisation; it is a family-like movement that believes in us, stands by us, and celebrates our success as its own.”
FROM A BASKETBALL PLAYER TO A SUB-INSPECTOR

Kalaivani

Sub-Inspector of Police

Kalaivani, 23, from Nagapattinam district, comes from a tsunami-affected family and has played state-level basketball. Mr. Kamal was her basketball coach at school; his guidance and Kalangarai's encouragement changed her life.

She joined Kalangarai IAS Academy in 2025 and cleared the Sub-Inspector of Police examination on her very first attempt. After joining the police in Nagapattinam district, she now serves as an SI in Chennai district.

SUCCESS IS CERTAIN WITH PERSEVERANCE

Rahuman

Sub-Inspector of Police · Vedaranyam

Rahuman, 29, from Thittachery, comes from a humble family — his father is a daily-wage labourer and his mother a homemaker. He began serious preparation in 2022, building his days around discipline, physical training and study at Kalangarai IAS Academy.

He cleared the 2024 SI examination and was posted to Vedaranyam.

“Our background does not define our future. With determination, discipline, perseverance and the right guidance, we can turn our dreams into reality.”
A GOVERNMENT CAREER WITHIN ONE YEAR

Karthik

Police – Armed Reserve, Madurai

Karthik, 29, from Madurai, came to Nagapattinam to support his elder brother, a Sub-Inspector, and joined Kalangarai on his advice. He prepared with complete dedication, cleared the examination in 2024 and received his appointment within a year of starting. He now serves in the Armed Reserve division in Madurai.

FROM A RURAL DREAM TO A JUDICIAL CAREER

Naveena

Tapedar, Nagapattinam Court

Naveena, from Sikkal village and a farmer's daughter, dreams of becoming an IAS officer. She received UPSC coaching at Kalangarai IAS Academy at an affordable cost, and won a Gold Medal at Tamil Nadu Dr. J. Jayalalithaa Fisheries University, presented by the Governor of Tamil Nadu.

She has now joined duty as a Tapedar at Nagapattinam Court, while continuing her UPSC journey.

FROM A FISHERMAN'S FAMILY TO PUBLIC SERVICE

Muthukkumar

Village Administrative Officer

Muthukkumar, 25, from tsunami-affected Keechankuppam — his father is a fisherman and his mother sells fish — studied full-time at Kalangarai IAS Academy. He cleared the examination in 2021 and serves as a Village Administrative Officer in Nagapattinam district.

INSPIRING A FAMILY THROUGH GOVERNMENT SERVICE

Sathish

Police – Armed Reserve, Nagapattinam

Sathish, 28, from Kadambadi, joined Kalangarai with his elder brother. Encouraged by the academy, he secured a government job within one year and now serves in the AR division in Nagapattinam — and his success has become a source of encouragement for his brother.

TURNING SETBACKS INTO SUCCESS

Santhosh

Fire & Rescue Services

Santhosh, 29, from Akkaraipettai, was born into a family affected by the 2004 tsunami. Unsuccessful in his first two attempts, he kept going and succeeded on the third. He serves in the Fire and Rescue Services Department in Nagapattinam district.

“As long as we continue to try, there is no end to our dreams.”
FROM AWARENESS TO ACHIEVEMENT

Mathivanan

Virudhachalam Co-operative Society

Mathivanan, 35, from Virudhachalam, moved to Nagapattinam when his wife was transferred there. A Kalangarai Competitive Exam Awareness Training changed his outlook; he joined for coaching, cleared the examination within a year and now works at the Virudhachalam Co-operative Society.

A MOTHER'S COURAGE

Farhana

Cleared the written examination

Farhana, 27, a mother of two, kept studying through the final months of her second pregnancy in 2024 and passed the examination. She did not clear the next selection stage — but she has not given up, and continues to pursue her dream with Kalangarai's support.

“No matter how difficult our circumstances may be, if we keep working without giving up on our dreams, we can certainly move forward.”
